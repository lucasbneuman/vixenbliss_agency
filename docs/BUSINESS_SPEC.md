# Business Specification — VixenBliss Agency

## 1. Objective

The purpose of this web application is to act as a high-conversion traffic funnel.

It must:

* attract users from social media
* retain attention through short-form video content
* drive users to external destination links
* maximize click-through rate

---

## 2. Product Definition

This is NOT a traditional website.

This is a:

> Vertical video web app optimized for conversion

It behaves like:

* TikTok / Reels UX (but simpler)
* No social features
* No user accounts
* No complexity

---

## 3. Core Principles

### 3.1 Simplicity

* No unnecessary pages
* No complex navigation
* Everything focuses on content consumption

---

### 3.2 Speed

* Instant load
* No friction
* No waiting

---

### 3.3 Addiction

* Continuous scroll
* Seamless transitions
* Always more content

---

### 3.4 Conversion

* Every piece of content has a goal:
  → drive clicks

---

## 4. User Experience

### 4.1 First Impression

* Dark UI
* Modern aesthetic
* Immediate video playback
* No clutter

---

### 4.2 Interaction Model

* Scroll vertically
* Videos autoplay
* User does not need to click to start

---

### 4.3 Content Loop

1. User watches video
2. Scrolls
3. Watches next
4. Repeats

---

### 4.4 CTA Strategy

Each video must include:

* a clear call-to-action
* subtle but visible
* not aggressive, but persuasive

Examples:

* “See more”
* “Unlock full content”
* “Continue watching”

---

## 5. Content Strategy

### 5.1 Content Type

* Short-form videos
* Visually engaging
* Suggestive but safe

---

### 5.2 Volume Strategy

* Constant content updates
* No empty states
* Feed should always feel alive

---

### 5.3 Ordering

Content must be:

* strategically ordered
* best-performing first
* newest content regularly injected

---

## 6. Marketing Workflow

Marketing team must be able to:

* upload videos to Bunny Stream
* create entries in Directus
* publish content easily

They must NOT:

* touch code
* deploy anything
* handle technical tasks

---

## 7. Conversion Optimization

### 7.1 Goals

* maximize click-through rate
* increase session duration
* increase scroll depth

---

### 7.2 Methods

* strong thumbnails
* compelling captions
* strategic CTA placement
* fast loading

---

## 8. Design Guidelines

* dark theme only
* minimal UI
* focus on content
* no distractions
* mobile-first

---

## 9. What This Product Is NOT

* not a social network
* not a CMS
* not a content platform
* not a marketplace

---

## 10. Success Criteria

The product is successful if:

* users scroll multiple videos
* users click CTA links
* users return
* content can be updated continuously without dev support

---

## 11. Long-Term Vision

* scale content volume
* test different creatives
* optimize conversion rates
* evolve into a traffic engine

---

## 12. Final Principle

Everything in this product must answer:

> “Does this increase conversion or engagement?”

If not, it should not exist.
